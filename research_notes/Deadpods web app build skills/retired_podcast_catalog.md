# Retired Podcast Seed Catalog for deadpods.com (status as of 2026-10-20)

**How this was checked.** Feed URLs come from the Apple iTunes Search API (`https://itunes.apple.com/search?entity=podcast&term=…`), which returns each show's `feedUrl`. On 2026-10-20 I fetched every candidate feed directly and recorded the item count, the oldest and newest item, and whether candidate "best episode" titles appear in it. "Last real ep" is the last actual episode of the show. Many dead feeds still get promo drops for other shows (e.g. "Introducing…"), so the feed's newest date is often _not_ the show's end date. Every feed URL below returned HTTP 200 with `<enclosure>` audio on 2026-10-20 unless marked otherwise. No feed URL was guessed.

Evidence key: **[feed]** = verified directly from the RSS feed on 2026-10-20 (titles, dates, counts). **[unsourced]** = editorial pick or general reputation with no critic or fan consensus source found this session. Treat these as a starting point, not consensus.

## Q1. Which well-regarded ended shows are still freely playable from public RSS? (main catalog)

### Takeaway

I verified **76 ended (or very likely ended) shows with full or near-full public RSS archives**, covering every requested genre. The surprise is that most of the Gimlet catalog is still public on Megaphone, including Mystery Show (all 6 cases), Reply All (214 items), Surprisingly Awesome, Every Little Thing, Uncivil, Sandra, The Habitat, The Two Princes and Motherhacker. The big exceptions are StartUp and Homecoming (see Q2).

### Cited Findings

**Seed example verified:** Mystery Show, `https://feeds.megaphone.fm/mysteryshow`, has 7 items: a trailer plus Case #1 Video Store, #2 Britney, #3 Belt Buckle (2015-06-05), #4 Vanity Plate, #5 Source Code and #6 Kotter (last item 2015-07-31) [feed]. It ran one season (May–July 2015). Apple named it Best New Podcast of 2015, and "Belt Buckle" was #1 on The Atlantic's top 50 podcast episodes of 2015 — [Wikipedia: Mystery Show](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mystery_Show). Gimlet let Starlee Kine go in April 2016 — [Wikipedia: Gimlet Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimlet_Media).

## Q2. Which network collapses removed catalogs, and what's still playable?

### Takeaway

Spotify/Gimlet is the main hole. Most cancelled Gimlet shows are still public on Megaphone, but **StartUp, Homecoming, Without Fail, Mogul and The Clearing have Apple Podcasts pages with no public `feedUrl`**, which points to Spotify-only. **The New York Times paywalled podcast archives in late 2024**, so its limited series (Caliphate, 1619, Rabbit Hole, Nice White Parents, Still Processing) now show only 1–3 free items. Stitcher's 2023 shutdown didn't kill Earwolf feeds.

### Cited Findings

- Spotify bought Gimlet in 2019, both founders left in 2022, and Wikipedia lists Gimlet as defunct in 2023 — [Wikipedia: Gimlet Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimlet_Media).
- In Oct 2022 Spotify cut 11 Gimlet/Parcast shows, including How to Save a Planet, Crime Show and Every Little Thing — [Siècle Digital](https://siecledigital.fr/2022/10/07/spotify-supprime-11-podcasts-originaux/). Six more went in June 2023 — [Radio Ink](https://radioink.com/2023/06/07/six-podcasts-cut-at-spotify-after-layoffs/). Heavyweight and Stolen were cancelled in Dec 2023 — [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2023/12/05/spotify-cancels-two-acclaimed-gimlet-podcasts/).
- Spotify "shut off the RSS feeds for many of its acquired and licensed podcasts", and some Gimlet shows lost about 75% of their audience when locked down — [Simon Owens](https://simonowens.substack.com:443/p/is-spotifys-podcast-exclusivity-strategy). Spotify later relaxed exclusivity for some Gimlet shows — [Podnews](https://Podnews.net/update/spotify-relaxes-exclusives).
- **No public feed in Apple's index (iTunes Search API, 2026-10-20):** StartUp Podcast (Apple id 913805339), Homecoming (1170934381), Without Fail (1437293054), Mogul (1247146656), The Clearing (1471432747, Pineapple Street/Gimlet). All return `feedUrl: null`. I didn't verify on Spotify directly that these are playable there.
- **Still public on Megaphone (verified 2026-10-20):** Mystery Show, Reply All, Surprisingly Awesome, Every Little Thing, Crimetown, Uncivil, Sandra, The Habitat, The Two Princes, Motherhacker, The Nod, How to Save a Planet (feeds in Q1).

- **NYT:** older episodes of NYT shows went subscriber-only (via Apple/Spotify, $6/mo or $50/yr) from about Oct 2024 — [Inside Radio](https://www.insideradio.com/free/new-york-times-to-put-podcast-archives-behind-new-paywall-new-episodes-will-stay-free/article_d7df0382-7c46-11ef-9c40-13c869002acd.html); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2024/09/24/new-york-times-audio-subscriptions-apple-spotify). Public feeds verified 2026-10-20:
  - Caliphate: 2 items (Prologue, Chapter One) — https://feeds.simplecast.com/Kaj9iZFl
  - 1619: 3 items (intro + 2 eps) — https://feeds.simplecast.com/M4uRd8jQ
  - Rabbit Hole: 3 items (START HERE, One, Two) — https://feeds.simplecast.com/rRMR9QHI
  - Nice White Parents (Serial/NYT): intro + Ep 1 only — https://feeds.simplecast.com/B07_EAQq

  - Modern Love feed shows 3 items. S-Town is still complete (7 chapters) even though it's Serial/NYT.
- **Stitcher** app and site shut down 2023-08-29. Earwolf and Stitcher Studios kept operating, and ad-supported shows moved to Pandora — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/06/27/stitcher-podcast-app-shutdown/), [TheWrap](https://thewrap.com/sirius-xm-stitcher-website-app-shutpdown-podcast). Earwolf's Analyze Phish is still public on Simplecast.

- The Gimlet shows with the strongest IP (StartUp, Homecoming → Amazon TV show) are the ones kept off public RSS. Expect them to stay that way.

## Q4. Which ended shows recur on critic and fan "finished podcasts" lists?

### Takeaway

I couldn't get a reliable cross-list frequency count. Vulture was blocked, and searches surfaced mostly SEO listicles. The recurring names that did surface were Serial, S-Town, Dr. Death and Dirty John as "classic self-contained series", plus Heavyweight and Ear Hustle in Reddit-derived roundups. Apple Podcasts ("Finished Series" collection), Spotify ("Completed Series") and Podchaser ("completed series" filter) are the better curation sources.

### Cited Findings

- Serial, S-Town, Dr. Death and Dirty John are cited as classic self-contained series; Podchaser has a "completed series" filter; Spotify has a "Completed Series" section; Apple has a "Finished Series" collection; "The End" is a directory of 1,000+ finished audio-fiction shows — per search-result summaries of [PodcastVideos](https://www.podcastvideos.com/end-to-end-podcast-series-binges/) and related listicles (low-quality aggregators, treat as weak).
- Reddit-derived roundups surface Heavyweight (now revived) and Ear Hustle (active) most often — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/tools/best-products/podcast-recommendations), [Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/these-podcasts-were-so-good-internet-users-couldnt-stop-listening) (aggregators, weak).
- Mystery Show is repeatedly praised as a one-season wonder: "the true mystery here is why this show only had one season" — [Quill review](https://www.quillpodcasting.com/podcast-review/mystery-show); "may be the best new podcast since Serial" — [Pajiba](https://www.pajiba.com/serial/the-mystery-show-may-be-the-best-new-podcast-since-serial.php).

### Inferences

- The fan "binge-worthy" canon is dominated by shows that are still active. Deadpods' real value is the truly ended, still-public archive, and Gimlet's Megaphone feeds plus WNYC/NPR/BBC limited series are the richest seams.

### Gaps

- No verified frequency counts from Vulture, The Atlantic, Esquire, Slate, the Guardian or r/podcasts. A follow-up should pull Apple's "Finished Series" collection and Podchaser's completed-series filter directly, ideally from a browser session, since those pages block simple fetches.
