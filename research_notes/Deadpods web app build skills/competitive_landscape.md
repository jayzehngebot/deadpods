# Competitive Landscape & Audience Demand for deadpods.com (as of Oct 2026)

Research method note: ~20 web searches/fetches. Reddit threads were **not reachable** through search tooling (no reddit.com results returned), so Reddit-specific demand evidence is a gap. Several "podcastvideos.com" claims are from a single secondary blog and are flagged.

## Existing sites, lists, and projects covering ended/finished podcasts

### Takeaway

The only dedicated, well-maintained "finished podcasts" directory found is **The End (theend.fyi)**, and it is **fiction/audio-drama only** and links out rather than playing audio. Everything else is scattered: undated listicles, user-made Podchaser lists, platform collections. No dedicated directory for finished **non-fiction** podcasts was found.

### Cited Findings

- **The End (theend.fyi)**: describes itself as "the only listener-focused directory for binge-ready fiction podcasts, audio dramas, and other audio fiction." Curated by Evo Terra (Simpler.media). Says it has "curated thousands of shows" and adds dozens weekly. Features: hand-curated collections (Horror, Sci-Fi, Fantasy, Family Time…), Fan Favorites, What's New, per-show labels ("Fully binge-ready," "Limited series," "New season underway"), letter grades, run time, listening links to apps, RSS/OPML exports, weekly email, a creator submit/update form. The homepage shows **no in-browser player, no best-episode highlights, and no sharing tools**. Updated as recently as Oct 20, 2026. — [The End](https://www.theend.fyi/)
- The End publishes year-based collections such as "Best Audio Fiction Shows Completed in 2022" and "…in 2024." — [The End 2022](https://www.theend.fyi/collection/best-completed-in-2022); [The End 2024](https://www.theend.fyi/collection/best-audio-fiction-shows-completed-in-2024)
- Other descriptions of The End give the size as "over 1,000" or "over 1,500" shows, which suggests the count has grown over time. — [podcastvideos.com](https://www.podcastvideos.com/end-to-end-podcast-series-binges/)
- **Podchaser user lists** exist, e.g. "Completed Audio Fiction Podcasts" (by juiceforpresident) and "Completed Audio Dramas" (by Ryan_Sliwinski). They are community-made, so coverage is uneven. — [Podchaser list 1](https://www.podchaser.com/lists/completed-audio-fiction-podcasts-107a4Yq4m1); [Podchaser list 2](https://www.podchaser.com/lists/completed-audio-dramas-107a98irzU)
- **Feedspot**: "40 Best Completed Audio Drama Podcasts You Must Follow in 2025," a ranked SEO list. — [Feedspot](https://podcast.feedspot.com/completed_audio_drama_podcasts/)
- **Podsauce**: "From the archives: 9 beloved, long-running podcasts that ended (but are still great listens)" includes The Anthropocene Reviewed (John Green), NPR's How to Do Everything, Bodega Boys, Wooden Overcoats, King Falls AM. Podsauce also runs "Done in a Day" pieces on short finished shows (e.g. Mystery Show). Could not fetch the page (cert error); details come from the search snippet. — [Podsauce](https://podsauce.com/articles/best-long-running-podcasts-that-ended/); [Podsauce Mystery Show](https://podsauce.com/articles/mystery-show-embarks-on-a-quest-for-truth-in-various-personal-stories)
- **Platform features (unverified, single secondary source)**: one blog says Spotify has a "Completed Series" section, Apple Podcasts has a "Finished Series Collection," and Podchaser has a "completed series" filter. I could not confirm these from official sources. — [podcastvideos.com](https://www.podcastvideos.com/end-to-end-podcast-series-binges/)
- **Apple's official move (Oct 2024)**: Apple added a new **Series** category for narrative/limited series and a monthly "Series Essentials" pick. This is the closest confirmed first-party feature. — [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/us-es/newsroom/2024/10/apple-podcasts-spotlights-narrative-series)
- The iTunes/Apple RSS tag `<itunes:complete>` lets publishers mark a show as complete. Listen Notes counts a show as "dead" when the feed is deleted or that "Completed" tag is set to yes. — [Listen Notes via search summary / DEV Community](https://dev.to/tonywangca/the-podcast-gold-rush-and-the-graveyard-you-cant-see-33ee)
- Generic "binge-worthy podcasts" listicles (The Everygirl, Grindr blog, Bed Threads 2021, Pedestrian) mix active and ended shows and are often undated. — [The Everygirl](https://theeverygirl.com/binge-worthy-podcasts/); [Grindr](https://www.grindr.com/blog/binge-worthy-podcasts); [Bed Threads 2021](https://bedthreads.com.au/blogs/journal/best-new-podcasts-2021)
- Major outlets (Vulture/Nicholas Quah, Esquire, The Atlantic) publish **year-end "best of" lists** (indexed 2019–2025) but no "best ended podcasts" list was found from them. — [yearendlists.com](https://www.yearendlists.com/category/podcasts); [Podcasts We Text About 2025 tally](https://podcastswetextabout.substack.com/p/the-top-10-podcasts-of-2025-mentioned?open=false)

### Inferences

- **Fiction is served (The End); non-fiction/documentary/chat finished shows are not.** That is deadpods' clearest open lane, or deadpods could cover both and still beat The End on playback and episode-level curation.
- Year-end lists from 2015–2022 are a ready source of candidates: many of those shows have since ended.
- The `itunes:complete` flag plus "last episode date" could support semi-automated discovery and status checks.

### Gaps

- Could not verify Spotify "Completed Series" / Apple "Finished Series Collection" / a Podchaser completed filter from first-party sources.
- No Reddit thread data retrieved (r/podcasts, r/podcastrecommendations). A manual Reddit search is recommended.
- No dedicated sites named "podcast graveyard," "finishedpodcasts," or "complete podcasts" surfaced in searches. That suggests none are prominent, but I did not check domains directly.
- Goodpods and Listen Notes curated lists of ended shows were not found or checked.

## Best-episode curation sites and "start here" features

### Takeaway

"Best episodes of X" exists only as scattered, aging SEO articles and as **per-show "Start Here" pages that each show maintains**. No cross-show site curating best/starter episodes was found, and these pages vanish when a show dies. That is a strong fit for deadpods.

### Cited Findings

- **Discover Pods** "The 14 Best Reply All Episodes" (published Feb 26, 2019, updated May 2019; title says 14 but lists 12). It has no embedded players and links out to Gimlet/Apple/Spotify. It runs a few other best-episode lists (e.g. Joe Rogan) alongside mostly general roundups. Picks include Long Distance I–II, The Russian Passenger, Exit & Return, Lost in a Cab, Zardulu, The Snapchat Thief. — [Discover Pods](https://discoverpods.com/best-reply-all-episodes/)
- Shows' own "start here" guides: Welcome to Night Vale ("over 100 episodes can seem a little intimidating"), This American Life ("New to This American Life?"), Reply All's "Where To Get Started" list, and Twenty Thousand Hertz (archive sorted by subject/emotion). — [Medium/Misener](https://medium.com/@misener/258fc3fbee85); [Podcast Marketing Magic](https://podcastmarketingmagic.substack.com/p/day-10-podcast-marketing-tips)
- **HeadStuff "Where Should I Start?"** series: a longtime listener picks entry episodes for a show (e.g. Stuff You Should Know). This is the closest editorial analog to deadpods' per-show best-episode format. — [HeadStuff SYSK](https://headstuff.org/?p=79753); [HeadStuff JaackMaate](https://headstuff.org/?p=80330)
- Podcast advisers recommend "start here" pages because they help new listeners into large back catalogs. — [Help Me Podcast E58](https://helpmepodcast.transistor.fm/episodes/e58-creating-a-start-here-page-to-keep-new-listeners)
- **Podcast Review (podcastreview.org)** publishes reviews, lists, essays, and a newsletter, including the July 2026 piece "Our Enduring Love For Mystery Show" with "what to listen to if you miss…" recommendations (The Renner Files, Strangers on a Bench, Decoder Ring). It shows active 2026 editorial interest in ended shows. — [Podcast Review](https://podcastreview.org/list/what-to-listen-to-if-you-miss-mystery-show/)
- Podcast marketers point out that finished shows keep drawing listeners from their archives. — [Podcast Marketing Magic](https://podcastmarketingmagic.substack.com/p/how-to-end-your-podcast-but-keep)

### Inferences

- When a show ends, its official "start here" page usually stops being maintained or goes offline with the show's site, while fans keep needing it. Deadpods can be the lasting "start here" for dead shows.
- "If you miss X, listen to Y" (Podcast Review's format) is a good secondary feature: related-show links between dead pods, and from dead pods to live ones.
- Existing best-episode articles have no inline players. Playing episodes in the browser and linking to a timestamp or episode are real differentiators.

### Gaps

- Did not check Podchaser episode-rating depth, Pocket Casts or Apple editorial "starter" features, or The Listener newsletter. Status unknown.

## Archive and preservation efforts for defunct podcasts

### Takeaway

Podcast loss is real and well documented, but preservation is mostly academic or one-off (the Internet Archive "2005 Podcast Core Sample," Preserve This Podcast). Corporate shutdowns (Stitcher, Spotify/Gimlet cuts) mostly **ended production rather than deleting feeds**, but availability is fragile and can change with rights holders.

### Cited Findings

- 2005 Podcast Core Sample: Jason Scott (now at the Internet Archive) captured ~14,000 episodes of 540 shows. In a sample of 125 shows from 2005, ~87% were no longer online and ~98% were gone from Apple Podcasts. — [Podnews press release](https://podnews.net/press-release/podcast-preservation)
- The Daily Source Code archive was recovered only because a fan had a full copy, which was shared over BitTorrent Sync. — [Podnews](https://podnews.net/press-release/podcast-preservation)
- **Preserve This Podcast** (2019, Molly Schwartz/Mary Kidd, METRO): a podcast plus workshops teaching indie podcasters preservation. It notes that industry changes such as hosting monetization and the closing of production departments threaten availability, and that few institutions collect podcasts. — [Archives AWARE 2022](https://archivesaware.archivists.org/2022/02/02/placing-archival-skills-into-the-hands-of-individual-podcasters-interview-with-mary-kidd-on-preserve-this-podcast/); [InfoDocket 2019](https://www.infodocket.com/2019/03/14/digital-preservation-a-new-podcast-and-national-series-of-workshops-on-podcast-preservation-launches-next-week/)
- **Stitcher** app and site closed Aug 29, 2023. Shows were said to stay available on other platforms, and Earwolf/Stitcher Studios continued under SiriusXM. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2023/06/27/stitcher-podcast-app-shutdown/); [AP via NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/ap-top-news/2023/06/27/siriusxm-is-shutting-down-its-stitcher-podcast-app-to-emphasize-its-flagship-app)
- **Spotify/Gimlet cuts**: 11 Gimlet and Parcast shows canceled Oct 2022 (incl. Crime Show, Every Little Thing); 6 more in Jun 2023 (incl. Gimlet's Conviction) when Gimlet and Parcast merged into Spotify Studios; Heavyweight and Stolen canceled Dec 2023. — [Variety](https://au.variety.com/2022/digital/news/spotify-cancels-podcast-shows-layoffs-6203/); [Radio Ink](https://radioink.com/2023/06/07/six-podcasts-cut-at-spotify-after-layoffs/); [MobileSyrup](https://mobilesyrup.com/2023/12/05/spotify-cancels-two-acclaimed-gimlet-podcasts/)
- In 2022, Gimlet, Ringer, and Parcast titles briefly disappeared from Spotify, which blamed "a technical issue." Spotify says availability "can vary over time and between countries, depending on the permissions from rights holders." — [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/joe-rogan-experience-spotify-gone-1302497/); [Spotify Community](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Content-Questions/Android-Where-are-old-episodes-of-a-podcast-I-just-started/m-p/5197790)
- **Dead pods can come back**: Heavyweight, canceled by Spotify in Dec 2023, was revived by Pushkin Industries (announced early 2025, back ~May 2025) and is distributed by iHeartPodcasts. — [Pushkin](https://www.pushkin.fm/news/jonathan-goldstein-joins-pushkin-industries-to-revive-acclaimed-heavyweight-podcast); [Podnews](https://podnews.net/update/heavyweight-pushkin-news); [Wikipedia](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Goldstein_(author)>)

### Inferences

- Deadpods should **embed or stream from the original RSS enclosure URLs** (not rehost; there are rights issues) and run a **link-health checker**. Flagging "audio no longer available" or "archived at Internet Archive" is itself useful and builds trust.
- Status should allow **"Ended," "Limited series (complete)," "On hiatus," "Revived"** (e.g. Heavyweight), since revivals happen.
- Linking to Internet Archive copies when the original feed is gone would be a unique preservation angle.

### Gaps

- Did not confirm the current RSS/Spotify availability of specific Gimlet catalog shows (Mystery Show, StartUp, Homecoming, Crimetown, Reply All). Reply All's Apple listing shows 214 episodes and appears to still be live. — [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/reply-all/id941907967)
- Nothing found on Luminary-exclusive shows' fate or on fan archives for specific network shutdowns.

## Search demand signals

### Takeaway

No public keyword-volume numbers for "podcasts that have ended" or "finished podcasts to binge" were found. Supply-side data, however, shows a huge and growing pool of dead shows, and there is steady editorial interest (2026 Podcast Review piece, year-based "completed" collections, Feedspot's 2025 SEO list). Demand appears to be niche but persistent.

### Cited Findings

- **Podcast Amplify study (Sep 2026)**: 39.1% of 14,915 topic-searched shows on Apple/Podcastindex had no new episode in over a year, versus only 6.3% of shows in Apple genre Top charts, because charts "can't show attrition." 81.9% of shows that stopped at 3 episodes are dead, versus 13.5% of shows with 100+ episodes. — [Talker News via Columbia Gorge News](https://www.columbiagorgenews.com/news/national/are-most-podcasts-dead-four-in-10-without-new-episode-in-a-year/article_e3dd42f0-3a63-5191-82f3-4242c888fa58.html)
- **Listen Notes / Inside Radio**: show deaths peaked at 117,124 in 2020; 39,398 in 2025. Only ~11% of the ~3.7M podcasts ever launched are currently active (2026 report). — [Inside Radio 2026](https://www.insideradio.com/free/fewer-new-podcasts-launching-in-2026-as-active-show-count-jumps/article_d851518f-0a25-41c3-8801-0b651c469331.html); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/tonywangca/the-podcast-gold-rush-and-the-graveyard-you-cant-see-33ee)
- A Semrush study (US, 2019–2022) found "serial podcast" had the largest drop among podcast queries, and SimpleTexting found "Serial" was the #2 most-searched podcast in the US. Interest in a famous finished show stays high but decays. — [Statista/Semrush](https://www.statista.com/statistics/1369066/ai-search-market-share/); [SimpleTexting](https://simpletexting.com/?p=7647)
- Article volume: "binge-worthy podcasts" listicles keep appearing from lifestyle and brand sites (2021–2025), which suggests ongoing SEO demand for the "binge" framing. — [Feedspot 2025](https://podcast.feedspot.com/completed_audio_drama_podcasts/); [Bed Threads 2021](https://bedthreads.com.au/blogs/journal/best-new-podcasts-2021)

### Inferences

- Search demand probably concentrates on **show-name queries** ("Reply All best episodes," "is Mystery Show coming back," "where to listen to X") more than generic "ended podcasts" queries. Per-show pages with clear titles ("Mystery Show: all 6 episodes, best first") are the SEO play.
- "Binge" / "complete" / "finished" framing outperforms "dead" for search, while "dead pods" works as a brand voice.

### Gaps

- No Google Trends or Keyword Planner numbers. Running these manually is recommended for: "podcasts that have ended," "finished podcasts," "completed podcasts," "binge podcasts," "[show] best episodes."

## Gaps and differentiation opportunities for deadpods.com

### Takeaway

No current competitor combines **(a) non-fiction plus fiction ended shows, (b) editor-picked best episodes, (c) in-browser playback, and (d) shareable episode links**. The End is closest but is fiction-only, show-level, and link-out. Best-episode articles are stale and have no players.

### Cited Findings

- The End: fiction only; no player, best-episode picks, or share features on the homepage. — [The End](https://www.theend.fyi/)
- Best-episode articles (e.g. Discover Pods 2019) are stale and link out with no players. — [Discover Pods](https://discoverpods.com/best-reply-all-episodes/)
- Platform availability is unstable and depends on rights holders. — [Spotify Community](https://community.spotify.com/t5/Content-Questions/Android-Where-are-old-episodes-of-a-podcast-I-just-started/m-p/5197790)
- Shows get revived (Heavyweight). — [Pushkin](https://www.pushkin.fm/news/jonathan-goldstein-joins-pushkin-industries-to-revive-acclaimed-heavyweight-podcast)
- There is editorial appetite for nostalgia/"golden era" framing (Mystery Show essay, 2026). — [Podcast Review](https://podcastreview.org/list/what-to-listen-to-if-you-miss-mystery-show/)

### Inferences (feature priorities)

1. **Per-show page = "Start Here" for a dead show**: 3–5 best episodes with a one-line why, total runtime, a "binge time" estimate, the end date, and why it ended.
2. **In-browser player from the original RSS enclosures**, plus share links to a specific episode (and ideally a timestamp) with good OG cards. Nobody in this space does this.
3. **Availability/health status**: feed live, Spotify-exclusive, paywalled, gone (with an Internet Archive link if one exists). Run automated link checks.
4. **Status taxonomy**: Ended / Complete limited series / Hiatus / Revived. Keep revived shows with a note.
5. **Non-fiction first** (documentary, chat, comedy, Gimlet-era), to avoid competing head-on with The End; link to The End for audio fiction.
6. **"If you miss X, try Y"**: related dead pods and live successors.
7. **Collections**: "Gimlet graveyard," "Done in a day (<6 hrs)," "One perfect season," "Ended 2022."
8. **SEO**: per-show and per-episode pages titled around show names and "best episodes."
9. Possible later: RSS/OPML export of picks (The End does this), user upvotes on best episodes.

### Gaps

- Reddit community sentiment and requests not captured.
- Did not assess licensing/ToS limits on embedding enclosure audio. Generally public RSS enclosures are playable, but this needs confirmation.
